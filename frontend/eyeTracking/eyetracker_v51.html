Webcam eye tracking

Runs 100% locally. Video never leaves your browser.

Sit about 50 to 70 cm away, face evenly lit (light in front of you, not behind), and keep your head fairly still. Look at each dot until it vanishes, then follow the moving dot for 10 s. Clicking anywhere while tracking also refines the model.
